Canonical billing rules

These rules are part of the product contract and should be implemented consistently across ReplyNodes billing.

  1. Each endpoint has a configurable credit_cost.
  2. Credit cost is configuration/database-driven, never hardcoded.
  3. Credits are charged only once per customer request.
  4. Internal retries never charge additional credits.
  5. 4xx, 5xx, provider failure, or blocked request = 0 credits.
  6. A valid successful response with zero results is billable.
  7. Each pagination request is a new billable request.
  8. Monthly plan credits reset every billing cycle.
  9. Monthly credits do not roll over.
  10. Paid plans may enable overage; Free cannot.
  11. Cache hits cost the same credits as non-cache hits.

Architecture contract

Meter capabilities, not vendors. Provider pricing can change without forcing a billing redesign.
